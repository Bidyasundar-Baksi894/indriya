# Indriya: The 3-Minute RISE Conclave Pitch Script

**Format:** 10 Slides + 1 Live Demo (Streamlit)
**Time Limit:** 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)
**Pacing:** Confident, slightly urgent, pausing only for effect.

---

### Slide 1: Title (0:00 - 0:10)
*(Slide shows INDRIYA logo and tagline: "We measure what AI can't see — the human mind using it.")*

**You:** "Hi everyone, I’m the Founder of Indriya. We are a computational behavioral science startup, and we build software that measures the one thing AI can’t see... the human mind using it."

### Slide 2: The Problem (0:10 - 0:35)
*(Slide shows bold text: AI works. The humans using it are failing.)*

**You:** "Enterprise AI is booming, but there is a massive, unspoken vulnerability. Companies are losing millions because human operators do one of two things: they either blindly trust AI outputs—which is **Automation Bias**—or they get overwhelmed and ignore the AI entirely—which is **Alert Fatigue**. Currently, there is absolutely no software layer that measures or fixes this cognitive disconnect in real-time."

### Slide 3 & 4: The Solution & LIVE DEMO (0:35 - 1:45)
*(Switch from slides to your Streamlit App: "1. Cognitive Bias Simulator" page)*

**You:** "Enter Indriya. We built the Algorithmic Trust Dashboard. It’s a B2B SaaS platform that continuously diagnoses cognitive bias in AI teaming. Let me show you how it works for a Cybersecurity SOC team."

*(Click 'Start Simulation')*
**You:** "Our engine embeds interactive micro-simulations into a worker's daily workflow. Let's look at this AI alert."

*(Wait for Alert 3: The Scanner)*
**You:** "Here, the AI is screaming 'CRITICAL' about a high-speed scan. But if the analyst looks closely, the hostname is our internal scanner. A rushed analyst blindly trusts the AI and clicks 'Block'." *(Click 'Block' on your screen)*. "That’s Automation Bias."

*(Wait for Alert 4: The Printer)*
**You:** "Now look at this one. The AI flags it as 'Low' confidence—just a printer error. But a printer sending an `.exe` file to the Domain Controller is a massive attack. If the analyst is fatigued, they click 'Ignore'." *(Click 'Ignore' on your screen)*. "That’s Alert Fatigue."

*(Switch to "2. Analyst Results" page)*
**You:** "Indriya instantly grades this behavior. As you can see, our dashboard flagged exactly where the analyst's cognitive vulnerabilities lie, calculating their precise 'Automation Bias Score'."

### Slide 5: How It Works (1:45 - 2:00)
*(Switch back to slides: ASSESS → DIAGNOSE → REMEDIATE)*

**You:** "We don't just diagnose; we remediate. Once we score the analyst, we trigger automated interventions—like dynamically adjusting the AI's alert thresholds or forcing Explainable AI checks—to recalibrate their trust before a breach happens."
