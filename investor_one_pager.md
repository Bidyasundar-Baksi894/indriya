# I N D R I Y A
### "We measure what AI can't see — the human mind using it."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

### THE PROBLEM
Companies deploying AI lose millions because human operators either blindly trust AI outputs (automation bias) or completely ignore them (algorithm aversion). Current systems lack the ability to measure this human-AI interaction breakdown.

### THE SOLUTION
Indriya is a B2B SaaS platform that deploys behavioral assessments and cognitive load simulations to diagnose and remediate human cognitive bias in AI-assisted decision-making. We ensure AI investments deliver ROI by aligning AI outputs with human decision-making processes.

### HOW IT WORKS
1. **ASSESS** → Deploy psychometric tasks to analysts and operators.
2. **DIAGNOSE** → Score automation bias, alert fatigue, and trust calibration.
3. **REMEDIATE** → Automated intervention playbooks & Explainable AI (XAI) recalibration.

### TARGET MARKET
*   **Cybersecurity SOC teams** (suffering from severe alert fatigue)
*   **Financial risk analysts** (prone to automation bias in algorithmic trading/lending)
*   **Smart manufacturing operators** (managing complex AI-driven IoT systems)

### BUSINESS MODEL
*   SaaS per-seat subscription for continuous behavioral monitoring.
*   Enterprise licensing tiers for large-scale deployments (500+ seats).

### TRACTION & VALIDATION
*   Working prototype of The Algorithmic Trust Dashboard.
*   Partnership discussions for validation studies on established psychometric literature.
*   Regulatory tailwinds from EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF demanding human-centric AI risk management.

### THE ASK
Raising a Seed round to fund a 12-month validation study, hire 2 core engineers, and secure 3 enterprise pilots in the cybersecurity and financial sectors.
